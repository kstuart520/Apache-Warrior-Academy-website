All Programs

FIND YOUR
PATH

Every program at AWA is built around a specific goal and student. Pick the one that fits — or reach out and we'll help you decide.

01
Ages 5–13

Kids Jiu-Jitsu

Most kids won't face a tournament — but they will face a bully, a hard day, or a moment that tests their character. Our youth program teaches BJJ as a tool for life: how to stay calm under pressure, respect others, and carry themselves with confidence.

  • Saturday 5:00–6:00 PM & Sunday 2:00–3:00 PM
  • Structured belt progression
  • Anti-bullying focus built into curriculum
  • Parent observation welcome
02
Ages 13+

Adult Jiu-Jitsu

You don't need experience — just the willingness to show up. Whether you're here to get fit, learn to defend yourself, or find something that challenges you like nothing else, adult BJJ at AWA meets you where you are and pushes you forward.

  • Saturday 6:00–7:00 PM & Sunday 3:00–4:00 PM
  • All skill levels welcome
  • Gi and no-gi training
  • Competition team eligibility
03
All Ages · By Invitation

Competition Team

For athletes who want to test what they've built. Competition training is structured, demanding, and not for everyone — and that's exactly the point. If you want to compete, we'll prepare you to walk onto that mat ready.

  • Invitation-based after foundational training
  • Tournament prep and live sparring
  • Strategy and game-plan development
  • Open to youth and adults
04
Community Workshops

Women's Self-Defense

Situational awareness, boundary-setting, and real escape techniques — no uniform required. This isn't a seminar full of hypotheticals. It's hands-on training built around the scenarios women actually face, taught in a respectful environment.

  • No prior experience needed
  • No gi required — street clothes welcome
  • Workshop and ongoing class options
  • Private group sessions available
05
Weekday Mornings

Homeschool Program

Physical education that actually develops something. Homeschool families get dedicated morning class times — structured BJJ instruction that builds focus, coordination, and the ability to work through difficulty alongside other kids.

  • Weekday morning scheduling
  • PE credit-compatible structure
  • Social development alongside training
  • Family-friendly rates available
06
LEO · Fire · EMS · Military

First Responder Training

Law enforcement, firefighters, and EMS deal with physical altercations in full gear, under stress, with lives on the line. This program teaches control techniques and defensive tactics built for those real-world conditions.

  • $20/month discount for qualifying personnel
  • Active duty, veterans, LEO, fire, EMS
  • Valid ID required at enrollment
  • Tactical control and restraint focus
