What We Offer

TRAINING
PROGRAMS

01

Kids Jiu-Jitsu

Most kids won't face a tournament — but they will face a bully, a hard day, or a moment that tests their character. Our youth program teaches BJJ as a tool for life: how to stay calm under pressure, respect others, and carry themselves with confidence.

Ages 5–17
02

Adult Jiu-Jitsu

You don't need experience — just the willingness to show up. Whether you're here to get fit, learn to defend yourself, or find something that challenges you like nothing else, adult BJJ at AWA meets you where you are and pushes you forward.

Ages 18+
03

Competition Team

For athletes who want to test what they've built. Competition training is structured, demanding, and not for everyone — and that's exactly the point. If you want to compete, we'll prepare you to walk onto that mat ready.

All Ages · By Invitation
04

Women's Self-Defense

Situational awareness, boundary-setting, and real escape techniques — no uniform required. This isn't a seminar full of hypotheticals. It's hands-on training built around the scenarios women actually face, taught in a respectful environment.

Community Workshops
05

Homeschool Program

Physical education that actually develops something. Homeschool families get dedicated morning class times — structured BJJ instruction that builds focus, coordination, and the ability to work through difficulty alongside other kids.

Weekday Mornings
06

First Responder Training

Law enforcement, firefighters, and EMS deal with physical altercations in full gear, under stress, with lives on the line. This program teaches control techniques and defensive tactics built for those real-world conditions — with discounted rates for those who serve.

LEO · Fire · EMS
